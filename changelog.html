Version history

What changed in each version, newest first.

  1. 1.0.5 9 November 2026

    • Three themes: Classic, Daylight and Blueprint, chosen for each widget.
    • Live updates: every few seconds while the printer heats up, calibrates, changes filament and in the last 10 minutes of a print.
    • Clearer states: paused, failed and offline stand out by colour and shape.

  2. 1.0.4 8 November 2026

    • Widgets can be resized freely. The layout adapts and the text grows to fill the space.
    • If Bambu Lab asks for a robot check, you can sign in on Bambu Lab's own page right in the app.

  3. 1.0.3 7 November 2026

    • The sign-in token and the printer access code are encrypted with a key that never leaves the phone.
    • The Multiple printers widget shows up to six printers.
    • Each widget remembers which printer it shows.
    • New time format.
