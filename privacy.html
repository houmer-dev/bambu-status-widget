Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Bambu Status Widget for Android (app.bambuwidget) · Developer: Jan Legát

In short

The app has no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. It sends data only to Bambu Lab, to sign you in and get your printer's status, and to your printer on your home network if you set that up. I never receive any of your data.

Security

All connections are encrypted. Everything the app stores stays in its private storage, which other apps can't read. The access token and the printer access code are also encrypted with a key that never leaves the phone, and they are left out of Android backups. The home Wi-Fi connection accepts only your own printer's certificate. If you sign in on Bambu Lab's own page, your password goes only to Bambu Lab.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared. Bambu Lab gets only what it needs to sign you in and send your printer's status, under its own privacy policy. The app connects to Bambu Lab the same unofficial way as other community tools, so a change on Bambu Lab's side may need an app update.

Deleting your data

Tap Sign out in the app to delete the sign-in, the printer details and the last status. Only your account email stays, to fill in the next sign-in. Uninstalling the app deletes everything. The access token also expires by itself after about 3 months. If you lose your phone, change your Bambu Lab password to end it right away. I keep no data on any server, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly handle their data.
