Your 3D print
on your home screen

An Android widget for Bambu Lab printers. See the time left, progress and loaded filaments without opening an app.

Join the test Features

Free, no ads. Android 12 or newer. Now in testing on Google Play.

What it shows

You choose what each widget shows.

Progress

A ring or bar that fills as the print goes. Paused, failed and offline stand out by colour and shape.

Time

Time left and the time the print will finish.

Filaments

All spools in the AMS and on the external holder. The one in use is marked.

Temperatures

Nozzle and bed temperature and the speed mode.

AMS humidity

See when your filament is getting damp.

Idle

Shows whether the last print finished or failed.

Live updates

Every few seconds while the printer heats up, calibrates, changes filament and in the last 10 minutes of a print. Every minute otherwise while your screen is on.

Themes

Six themes, chosen for each widget. Material takes your phone's colours and follows its dark mode.

Shapes and setup

Pick a rectangle, a circle or a strip in one of seven sizes, right in the settings. With several printers, one widget can show up to six. Pick one of six themes and make the background as transparent as you like.

  1. Install the app and open it.
  2. Sign in with your Bambu Lab account, the same one you use in Bambu Handy. Google and Apple accounts work too.
  3. Choose a shape and tap Place the widget.

Tap the widget to refresh it. Tap the camera icon to open Bambu Handy.

FAQ

Why doesn't the camera button open the right screen in Bambu Handy?

Open the Bambu Handy settings and set Home Page to Devices.

Is the widget secure?

Yes. You sign in to Bambu Lab, just as in Bambu Handy. Your password goes only to Bambu Lab and is never stored. The app keeps only the sign-in token that Bambu Lab sends back, and it is encrypted on your phone. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else.

Is the widget free?

Yes, and it will stay free. If you like it, please consider supporting further development.

Can I use it without signing in to Bambu Lab?

Yes. You can connect directly to your printer over your home Wi-Fi. The widget then updates only when your phone is on that Wi-Fi.

Does it work with other 3D printer brands?

No, the widget is made only for Bambu Lab printers.

Will there be more visual styles?

There are six themes now: Classic, Daylight, Material, Sand, Neon and Candy. Material uses your phone's own colours and follows its dark mode.

Will there be an iOS version?

No, sorry.
